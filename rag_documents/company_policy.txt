Company Policy

Working Hours:
Employees work from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday.

Leave Policy:
Employees receive 20 paid leave days per year.
Planned leave should be requested at least 3 days in advance.

Work From Home:
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval.

Employee Benefits:
Employees receive health insurance and access to professional training programs.
